The Research Table

How it works
  • Activity 1 · Write an Intro. Teams draw an education level and a topic, then write a hook, background, problem and purpose, using your examples as models.
  • Activity 2 · Guess the Research Questions. Teams pick an article, read its abstract and the passage leading up to its research questions, write their guess, and flip a card to reveal the real questions.
  • Both activities end with a payout ticket image students can download or print to submit.
  • Your setup saves automatically in this browser. To edit a finished activity later, choose Open an activity and select its downloaded file.

Starter articles are real open-access studies reproduced under CC BY 4.0, with the citation, link and license shown to students.

Everything you enter is built into the file you download, so only use material you have the right to share.
